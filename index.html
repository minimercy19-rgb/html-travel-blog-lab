Adventures in Fire Island

Known for its car-free environment, pristine beaches, and diverse communities, Fire Island is a perfect destination for those seeking a peaceful retreat from the hustle and bustle of city life.
Key Features of Fire Island include:

  • Car-Free Environment: The absence of motor vehicles leaves the air clean.
  • The Sunken Forest: A rare, pristine maritime holly forest tucked behind the sand dunes, accessible via a winding nature boardwalk.
  • Pristine National Seashore: Miles of protected, sweeping white-sand beaches ideal for surfing, shell hunting, and catching spectacular sunsets.
